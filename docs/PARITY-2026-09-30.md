# SettleKit parity audit (2026-09-30)

Branch: `parity/2026-09-30` from `main` at `b021180`. This is a code audit, not a docs audit: every row below cites the file that proves the gap.
Competitors compared: Stripe stablecoin payments, Coinbase Commerce, Helio, NOWPayments, Request Finance, Sphere. Their names stay in this internal doc only. None of them may appear in public copy.

Ranking rule: how much the gap blocks the **first paying merchants**. That means (a) SettleKit actually collecting its fee, (b) the first flows a merchant tries (bill a client, run a promo, embed a button), and (c) the bookkeeping they need before they can put real revenue through it.

Guardrails for every build item:
- Additive only. The Colosseum flow (`docs/colosseum/*`: Solana Pay checkout, x402, onchain subscriptions) and the Tameion flow (`docs/tameion/*`: Arc operator) must keep working with defaults unchanged. New checkout behaviour sits behind optional fields, so an existing session request stays valid.
- OSS first. The candidates are listed per gap with their licences checked on 2026-09-30 through `gh api`. Record the source repo and commit in `docs/OSS.md` when anything is vendored.

## What is already at parity (verified in code)

- Multi-chain USDC checkout: Solana Pay, EVM, Arc, Tempo, HyperCore and Zcash. Files: `apps/checkout/components/*Pay.tsx` and `apps/api/src/routes/checkout-sessions.ts`.
- Automatic access delivery: GitHub, Discord, files, license keys, API keys and SaaS entitlements. The delivery worker jobs have retries.
- Reusable payment links: `/l/[slug]` in `apps/api/src/merchant/payment-links.ts`.
- Onchain subscriptions with a buyer self-cancel page: `/s/[token]` and `routes/onchain-billing.ts`.
- x402 (EVM and SVM) and the MPP agent payment rails.
- Postgres persistence for every commerce store: coupons, invoices, refunds, disputes, dunning and payouts (`apps/api/src/context.ts` `pick(...)`).
- Signed seller webhooks. The Postgres outbox is retried by `apps/worker/src/jobs/webhook-retry-job.ts`.
- A customer portal with purchases, access, keys, downloads and subscriptions (`apps/portal`).
- Dashboard pages for coupons, invoices, refunds, disputes, dunning, payouts, analytics (revenue and MRR) and webhooks.
- SDKs in TypeScript (`packages/sdk` and `packages/react`), Python, Go and Rust.
- Circle Mint mint and redeem (`routes/mint.ts`), which is an institutional off-ramp.

## Ranked gaps

| # | Gap | Evidence in code | Competitor bar | Build (OSS first) | Priority |
|---|-----|------------------|----------------|-------------------|----------|
| G1 | **SettleKit's own fee is not collected on direct-to-wallet payments** | `apps/web/app/pricing/page.tsx` advertises "fee per successful payment, funds land in your own wallet". But `totalPlatformFees` is only used in `routes/payouts.ts` (the custodial payout path). Checkout sends 100% to the merchant's `payToAddress`, and `packages/pricing` (`HOSTED_PLAN_LIMITS`, `canCreateProduct`) is not imported by any app. | Every competitor takes its fee at settlement. | A monthly platform-fee statement, built as an invoice from confirmed payments × `platformFeeSchedule`. The merchant pays it through SettleKit's own checkout (dogfood). After a grace period, plan limits are enforced (`packages/pricing`). An optional later step is an onchain fee split through the existing `contracts/src/RecursiveSplitDistributor.sol` on EVM. | 1 |
| G2 | **Invoices and payment requests cannot be paid onchain** | `routes/invoices.ts` `POST /:id/pay` only flips the status by hand. There is no public invoice URL, no checkout session bound to the invoice, no chain verification and no email send. Payment links (`merchant/payment-links.ts`) only work for catalog products, so a one-off amount request is impossible. | Request Finance, Coinbase Commerce and Helio all have "send an invoice or request, client pays in USDC, status auto-updates". | Add `POST /v1/invoices/:id/send`. It creates a checkout session from the invoice total (with tax), stores `checkoutSessionId` on the invoice and emails the link through the existing `packages/notifications` / `email-templates`. On `payment.confirmed` for that session, the invoice is marked paid automatically (worker hook). Add a public `/i/[token]` page in `apps/checkout`, plus an ad-hoc "payment request" (amount, memo, payer email) built on the same path. Generate the PDF with **foliojs/pdfkit (MIT)**. Model the lifecycle on **btcpayserver/btcpayserver (MIT)** invoices and payment requests. | 1 |
| G3 | **Coupons never reach checkout** | The coupon engine, API and dashboard exist (`routes/coupons.ts`). But `routes/checkout-sessions.ts` `createSchema` has no coupon field. `apps/checkout` has no promo input (grep for coupon or discount finds nothing), and `payment-links.ts` ignores coupons. | Every competitor offers promo codes at checkout. | Add an optional `couponCode` on session create, an optional `?promo=` on `/l/[slug]` and a promo field in `OrderSummary`. Validate with `CouponService.validate`, store `discount` on the session so the verifier expects the discounted amount, and redeem on confirm. Recompute the Zcash and Solana amount bindings after the discount. No OSS is needed because the engine is already in the repo. | 2 |
| G4 | **No tax at checkout, and receipts are not tax-grade** | `packages/tax` `calculateTax` is imported by no app. Tax exists only on invoices. The buyer fields (`BuyerFields.tsx`) collect no billing country, address or VAT ID, and there is no merchant tax ID on receipts. The portal "Purchases & receipts" page shows only the tx hash, with no downloadable receipt or invoice PDF. | Tax-inclusive receipts and invoice PDFs are available in the customer portal. | Add per-product or per-org tax rates (org settings), billing country and VAT ID fields at checkout, and apply `calculateTax` in the session total. The receipt PDF is shared with G2 (pdfkit) and gets a download link in the portal purchases page and the receipt email. | 2 |
| G5 | **No accounting export for merchants** | `packages/exports` `toCsv` is only used by `apps/admin` (the operator view). There is no merchant CSV route and no Xero or QuickBooks connector. | CSV plus Xero and QuickBooks sync are a key selling point for B2B USDC billing. | Phase 1: add `GET /v1/exports/{payments,refunds,invoices,payouts}.csv` for the merchant, with a dashboard download button. Phase 2: an OAuth connector that pushes paid invoices and payments with **XeroAPI/xero-node (MIT)**. For QuickBooks, use **intuit/oauth-jsclient (Apache-2.0)** plus the QBO REST API (`mcohen01/node-quickbooks` has no SPDX licence, so do not vendor it). | 3 |
| G6 | **No embeddable checkout widget and no e-commerce plugin** | The "Embed a button" docs (`apps/web/app/docs/integrate/page.tsx`) are a plain `<a href>`. `packages/react` `UpgradeButton` is React-only. There is no drop-in JS overlay or inline iframe and no WooCommerce or Shopify plugin. | A JS widget or overlay plus WooCommerce, Shopify and other plugins. | Build `apps/checkout/public/embed.js`: a small script that opens `/l/[slug]` or a session in an overlay iframe and relays success through `postMessage`, with an allow-listed origin. Port a WooCommerce gateway from **btcpayserver/woocommerce-greenfield-plugin (MIT; WordPress distribution needs GPL-compatible, and MIT is)** and swap its API client for `/v1/checkout-sessions` plus webhook verification. | 3 |
| G7 | **Pricing is USDC-only, with no fiat display or live FX** | `packages/common/src/money.ts` has `type Currency = "USDC"`. `routes/fx.ts` is a USDC/EURC StableFX quote that needs the caller to supply the rate. | Price in USD, EUR or GBP, the buyer sees the local amount, and settlement is in a stablecoin. | Add an optional `displayCurrency` on prices. A rate service is fed by a self-hosted **lineofflight/frankfurter (MIT, ECB rates)** container, or the fetched API as a fallback. The quote is locked on the session with a TTL, as the Zcash quote already is. Settlement stays in USDC (and EURC on Arc). | 4 |
| G8 | **No webhook event log with resend and test** | The dashboard webhooks page shows *delivery-runs* (access delivery), not per-endpoint webhook attempts. There is no `POST /v1/webhooks/events/:id/resend`, no send-test-event, no secret rotation and no disable of a failing endpoint. `packages/webhook-replay` is not imported. | An event log with request and response per attempt, plus replay. | Expose the `worker_webhook_jobs` attempts per event (status code, error, next retry). Add resend (queue a fresh outbox job with `packages/webhook-replay`), a test event, secret rotation (with a grace window where both secrets are valid) and auto-disable after N consecutive failures. Add a dashboard table and a drawer. | 4 |
| G9 | **No team roles, and API key scopes are not enforced** | `packages/api-keys` `hasScope` exists, but `middleware/auth.ts` never checks scopes per route, so every key is effectively full access. `packages/invitations` is unused. There are no org members or roles in `packages/auth`, and each org has one login. | Owner, admin, developer and support roles, plus restricted keys. | Add org members (owner/admin/developer/viewer) with invite by email (wire up `packages/invitations` and the magic-link path). Add a route-to-scope map in the auth middleware (e.g. `payments:read`, `refunds:write`, `webhooks:write`) and a restricted-key UI. | 5 |
| G10 | **Refunds are record-only** | `merchant.ts` `POST /payments/:id/refund` records a refund with a merchant-supplied `txHash`. Nothing helps build or sign the refund transfer back to the payer. The vendored `apps/refund-protocol` has a known arbiter-drain issue in its README and is not wired. | A one-click refund to the original payer address. | Build a "refund to payer" action that prepares an unsigned USDC transfer (Solana Pay transfer-request URL or EIP-681 or wallet-connect tx) to the payer address recorded on the payment. The merchant signs it in the dashboard, the tx is verified onchain with the existing verifiers, and only then is the refund marked succeeded. Do not wire `refund-protocol` until its upstream fix lands. | 5 |
| G11 | **No fiat off-ramp hand-off for small merchants** | The only off-ramp is Circle Mint (`routes/mint.ts`, which needs an institutional `CIRCLE_MINT_API_KEY`). | A cash-out-to-bank hand-off. | Add a hand-off page in payouts that deep-links to supported off-ramp partners with the amount, network and wallet prefilled (no custody). The partner agreements and keys have to come from the owner. | 6 |
| G12 | **The hosted storefront is thin** | `apps/marketplace/app/sellers/[slug]` lists a seller's items, but it has no merchant branding, no custom domain, no product grid with a cart or bundle checkout, and no SEO fields. | A branded store page per merchant. | Extend the seller page with branding from the merchant profile (logo, colour), multi-item checkout (checkout already accepts multiple `items`) and an optional custom domain through the existing Railyard or Caddy routing. Borrow component ideas from **medusajs/medusa** only after checking its licence: GitHub reports NOASSERTION, the repo says MIT, and this must be checked before copying. | 6 |
| G13 | **SDKs are not published** | `@settlekit/sdk` and `@settlekit/react` are at `0.0.0`. The Python, Go and Rust SDKs have no release tags. | Installable SDKs on npm, PyPI, crates and Go modules. | Add versioning and publish workflows. Publishing needs owner registry credentials. | 7 |
| G14 | **Checkout and analytics depth** | Checkout is English-only (`packages/localization` is unused). Analytics has revenue and MRR but no churn, LTV, conversion (sessions to paid) or per-link stats. | Localized checkout, conversion and churn charts. | Localize the checkout strings through `packages/localization` (en, es; no Turkish marketing pages). Add conversion and churn metrics computed from sessions and subscriptions in `routes/analytics.ts`. | 8 |

Disputes: stablecoin payments have no card chargebacks. The existing `disputes` module (evidence and resolve) is enough for internal case tracking. **No gap to build.**

## Build order proposal

1. G1 platform fee statement and G2 payable invoices share one path: invoice → checkout session → auto-mark-paid. Build that path once.
2. G3 coupons, then G4 tax plus receipt PDF (G4 reuses the G2 PDF renderer).
3. G5 CSV export, then G6 embed.js and the WooCommerce port.
4. G8 webhook log, G9 roles and scopes, G10 refunds, then G7 FX.
5. G11 to G14 depend on owner inputs (partners, registries) or have lower impact.

## Owner-only inputs

- Confirm the fee model for G1: a monthly statement, an onchain split, or both.
- The off-ramp partner choice and keys (G11).
- npm, PyPI and crates publish credentials (G13).
- Xero and Intuit developer app registrations (G5, phase 2).
